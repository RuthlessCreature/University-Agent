This teaching example describes flipper-length differences between female and male Adelie penguins in the public palmerpenguins dataset. Of 152 Adelie records, 146 had complete sex and flipper-length data, with 73 in each group. Mean flipper length was 192.41 millimetres in males and 187.79 millimetres in females, a male-minus-female difference of 4.62 millimetres. Resampling records with replacement within each group 10,000 times gave a percentile interval of 2.68 to 6.58 millimetres for this difference. This interval assumes independent records, an assumption not established from the simplified dataset. Missing-data mechanisms, nesting dependencies and the target population remain unresolved. The comparison demonstrates descriptive reporting of the included records; it does not establish a causal effect or a general difference across all penguins.
